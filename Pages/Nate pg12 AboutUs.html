About Us

We are Microslop Game Studios, a passionate team of developers dedicated to pushing the boundaries of interactive entertainment. With a shared love for complex mechanics and compelling narratives, our journey began with a simple mission: to create games we wanted to play ourselves. From humbling beginnings to our latest projects, GACHARANT represents our commitment to innovation and player-driven experiences. We believe in transparency, community collaboration, and the power of great design.

Why Should You Play This Game

A video game for some people is a place to escape from the world and stop thinking about it, a place to immerse in the virtual world. In GACHARANT you get to immerse yourself in a chaotic 32 v 32 battle against other players eliminating each other with a wide variety of unique weapons for a different experience. We hope that you enjoy GACHARANT and have countless hours of memorises playing this game.
